Expiry Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 | Last Updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

At Expiry Tracker, we respect your privacy. Expiry Tracker is designed as a local-first expiry and reminder management application. We believe your data belongs to you, which is why our app is built to work entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection for its core functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Expiry Tracker does not require an account or registration. We do not intentionally collect, transmit, or upload any personal information to our servers.

To provide its functionality, you may voluntarily enter information into the app, such as:

  • Item names or titles
  • Categories
  • Expiry and renewal dates
  • Notes
  • Reminder preferences
  • Attachments (photos or files) selected by you

All of this information remains strictly on your device.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

All app data, including entered text, selected dates, and attachments, is stored locally on your device's internal storage.

The developer does not have direct access to this locally stored information. Please note that uninstalling the app or clearing its app data from your device settings may permanently remove your locally stored information.

4. Notifications

Expiry Tracker uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming expiry dates. These notifications are generated directly by your device's operating system. No expiry information is sent to a remote server to process or trigger these reminders.

5. Photos, Camera, and Files

If you choose to add an attachment to an expiry item, Expiry Tracker may request permission to access your device's camera, photo library, or file system, depending on the feature you select.

Any files or photos you select are used solely for the requested functionality within the app and are saved locally on your device. We do not upload or analyze your photos or files remotely.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of Expiry Tracker does not use backend services, analytics platforms (such as Firebase Analytics), or any external cloud databases. Your app operates independently.

7. Data Sharing

Because your data is not transmitted to us, we do not sell, rent, or share your stored expiry information with any third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other purposes.

8. Data Security

Your data's security relies on the protections provided by your device's operating system. We recommend using standard local device security mechanisms, such as screen locks, passcodes, and biometric authentication (like Face ID or fingerprint scanners), to help protect the information stored locally by the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Expiry Tracker is a general utility app not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored data remains on your device until you decide to delete it. You can delete specific items within the app, clear the app data through your device settings, or uninstall the app completely.

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, we cannot remotely recover or delete it for you.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the app's functionality or our privacy practices change. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
